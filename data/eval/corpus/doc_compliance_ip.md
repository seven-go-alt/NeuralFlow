# Meridian Analytics — Intellectual Property Policy

**Document ID:** doc_compliance_ip
**Owner:** Legal and Compliance
**Last updated:** 2026-02-28

## Purpose

This document defines Meridian Analytics' policy regarding intellectual property ownership, including whether intellectual property developed by an employee outside of work hours using company resources can be claimed by the company. The policy establishes clear boundaries between company-owned IP and employee-owned IP to prevent disputes and ensure compliance with applicable laws.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and consultants of Meridian Analytics. It covers all forms of intellectual property, including patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, software, algorithms, documentation, inventions, discoveries, and improvements conceived or developed during the term of employment or engagement with Meridian.

## Ownership of intellectual property developed using company resources

If intellectual property is developed by an employee outside work hours using company resources, the policy states that the company may claim ownership of that IP, subject to certain conditions. Specifically, Meridian Analytics asserts ownership rights over any intellectual property that meets any of the following criteria:

- The IP is developed using company resources, including laptops, servers, software licenses, cloud infrastructure, data sets, or any other Meridian-provisioned tools or materials.
- The IP relates to Meridian's current or reasonably anticipated business, research, or development activities.
- The IP results from work that is within the scope of the employee's role or responsibilities at Meridian.

However, if an employee develops intellectual property entirely outside of work hours, without using any company resources (including personal equipment, personal software licenses, and personal internet connection), and the IP is unrelated to Meridian's business, the intellectual property belongs to the employee. Employees who wish to retain ownership of outside IP should seek a written determination from the Legal team before development begins.

## Employee obligations

All employees are required to:

1. Disclose any inventions or intellectual property conceived or developed during their employment, regardless of whether company resources were used. Disclosures are submitted through the IP Disclosure Form in the Legal portal.
2. Execute an Assignment of Inventions agreement as a condition of employment, which assigns to Meridian all IP developed within the scope of employment or using company resources.
3. Refrain from using Meridian's confidential information or trade secrets to develop personal intellectual property.
4. Notify Legal before accepting any outside employment, consulting engagement, or board position that may involve intellectual property creation.

## Pre-existing IP

Employees may retain ownership of intellectual property developed prior to joining Meridian, provided that it is listed on the Prior Inventions schedule attached to their employment agreement. Meridian is granted a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any pre-existing IP that is incorporated into Meridian products.

## Dispute resolution

Disputes regarding intellectual property ownership are escalated to the Chief Legal Officer for resolution. If the dispute involves a legal claim, it is referred to external counsel. Meridian encourages employees to proactively discuss potential IP ownership questions with the Legal team before beginning development work that involves personal time or resources.
