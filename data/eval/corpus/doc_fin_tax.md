# Corporate Income Tax Rate for C-Corporations Under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act

**Document ID:** MER-FIN-2026-004
**Owner:** Tax Compliance Department
**Last Updated:** July 15, 2026

## Overview

This memorandum documents the current corporate income tax rate applicable to C-corporations following the enactment of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) of 2017. Meridian Analytics is structured as a C-corporation for federal income tax purposes, and this rate governs all federal corporate income tax calculations for the company.

## Current Tax Rate

The corporate income tax rate for C-corporations under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act is a **flat 21%**. This rate applies to all taxable income earned by the corporation, regardless of the amount. The TCJA eliminated the prior graduated rate structure that ranged from 15% to 35%, replacing it with a single flat rate.

The flat rate structure means that there is **no income threshold** for the 21% rate. Unlike the individual income tax system, which features multiple brackets and phase-outs, the corporate rate is uniform across all income levels. A C-corporation with $100,000 of taxable income pays at 21%, and a C-corporation with $100 million of taxable income also pays at 21%.

## Comparison to Pre-TCJA Structure

Prior to the TCJA, C-corporations were subject to a graduated rate structure with the following brackets:

- 15% on the first $50,000 of taxable income
- 25% on income between $50,001 and $75,000
- 34% on income between $75,001 and $10,000,000
- 35% on income over $10,000,000

Additionally, the pre-TCJA structure included a phase-out of the lower brackets for corporations with taxable income over $100,000, creating an effective marginal rate of 39% in certain ranges. The TCJA's flat 21% rate simplified compliance and significantly reduced the tax burden for most C-corporations.

## Impact on Meridian Analytics

As a privately held C-corporation, Meridian Analytics calculates its federal income tax provision using the 21% flat rate on pre-tax book income, adjusted for permanent and temporary differences. The flat rate simplifies deferred tax asset and liability calculations because the enacted rate is not expected to change with income level.

## Effective Tax Rate Considerations

While the statutory federal rate is 21%, Meridian's effective tax rate may differ due to:

- State and local income taxes (which are deductible for federal purposes)
- Permanent differences such as meals and entertainment disallowances
- Research and development tax credits
- Executive compensation limitations under Section 162(m)

## Conclusion

Meridian Analytics applies the 21% flat corporate income tax rate for C-corporations under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, with no income threshold applicable. This rate governs all federal income tax computations for the company.
