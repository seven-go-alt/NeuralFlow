# Meridian Analytics — IT Asset Provisioning Policy

**Document ID:** doc_it_provisioning
**Owner:** IT Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-01-28

## Purpose

This document describes the standard procedure to provision a new employee laptop through the IT asset management system at Meridian Analytics. A consistent provisioning process ensures that every new hire receives a secure, fully configured device on or before their first day, minimizing downtime and maintaining compliance with the company's security standards.

## Scope

This policy applies to all new full-time employees, contractors, and interns who require a company-managed laptop for their role. Provisioning requests must be submitted by the hiring manager through the IT asset management system at least 5 business days before the employee's start date. Same-day provisioning requests are handled on a best-effort basis but are strongly discouraged.

## Step-by-step provisioning procedure

The following steps outline the complete process to provision a new employee laptop through the IT asset management system:

1. **Manager submits request:** The hiring manager creates an asset request in the IT asset management system, selecting the appropriate laptop model based on the employee's role and department. Standard models include the Meridian Standard Laptop (Engineer), the Meridian Business Laptop (non-engineering), and the Meridian Workstation (Data Science).

2. **IT reviews and approves:** The IT Operations team reviews the request within 24 hours. If the request is complete and the budget is confirmed, IT approves it and assigns a device serial number in the asset management system.

3. **Device imaging and configuration:** IT prepares the laptop by installing the company-standard operating system image, which includes encryption software, antivirus, VPN client, MDM enrollment, and the Meridian productivity suite. This step typically takes 2 to 4 hours.

4. **Security compliance check:** Before release, the device undergoes an automated compliance scan to verify that full-disk encryption is enabled, the OS is patched to the latest version, and all security agents are reporting correctly.

5. **Asset registration:** The laptop is registered in the IT asset management system with the employee's name, department, start date, and assigned accessories (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset). The asset tag is affixed to the device.

6. **Delivery and onboarding:** The laptop is either shipped to the employee's home address (for remote hires) or made available for pickup at the office. IT schedules a 30-minute onboarding call to walk the employee through first-time setup, password changes, and MFA enrollment.

7. **Post-provisioning verification:** Three business days after delivery, IT confirms that the employee has completed all onboarding steps and that the device is reporting correctly in the asset management system.

## Device life cycle management

Each laptop is assigned a standard life cycle of three years. After 36 months, the device is flagged for replacement by the IT asset management system. Employees due for a refresh receive an automated notification 60 days before the scheduled replacement date. Retired devices are wiped, decommissioned in the asset management system, and processed through Meridian's certified e-waste vendor.

## Exceptions

Requests for non-standard hardware or expedited provisioning must be approved by the IT Operations Manager. Budget exceptions require approval from the department VP and Finance.
