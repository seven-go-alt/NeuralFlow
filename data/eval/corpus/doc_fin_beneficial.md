# Accounting Memorandum: Beneficial Ownership Information Reporting Under the Corporate Transparency Act

## Overview of the Corporate Transparency Act Requirements

The Corporate Transparency Act (CTA), effective January 1, 2024, requires certain entities formed or registered in the United States to report their beneficial ownership information (BOI) to the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN). This memorandum summarizes the filing obligations for a Delaware limited liability company (Delaware LLC) formed after the effective date, with particular emphasis on entities formed during the 2025 calendar year.

## Filing Timeline for a Delaware LLC Formed in 2025

For a Delaware LLC formed on or after January 1, 2024, the BOI report must be filed with FinCEN within 90 calendar days of the date of formation. An entity formed in 2025 enjoys a 90-day window from the date its formation documents are accepted by the Delaware Secretary of State. This filing window applies uniformly to all domestic reporting companies, including LLCs, corporations, and similar entities created by filing a document with a secretary of state or similar office. Failure to file within the 90-day period may result in civil and criminal penalties.

## Identifying Beneficial Owners

A beneficial owner is any individual who, directly or indirectly, exercises substantial control over the reporting company or owns or controls at least 25 percent of the ownership interests of the reporting company. For a Delaware LLC, ownership interests may be structured as membership interests, capital accounts, or profit-sharing arrangements. The CTA requires the reporting company to identify each beneficial owner by providing their full legal name, date of birth, complete current residential or business street address, and a unique identifying number from an acceptable identification document such as a passport or state-issued driver's license.

## Required Information for BOI Reports

Each BOI report submitted to FinCEN must contain the following information for the reporting company itself: its full legal name, any trade or doing-business-as names, its business street address, its jurisdiction of formation, and its IRS taxpayer identification number. For each identified beneficial owner, the reporting company must provide the individual's name, birthdate, address, and the identification number from a non-expired passport, state driver's license, or other acceptable identification document, along with an image of the document. FinCEN will retain this information in a secure non-public database accessible only to authorized government agencies and financial institutions conducting customer due diligence.

## Compliance Considerations

Companies formed in 2025 should maintain accurate and current records of their beneficial ownership structure and promptly file any updates or corrections to previously submitted BOI reports. Changes in beneficial ownership, such as transfers of membership interests or changes in control, require an updated BOI report within 30 calendar days. Meridian Analytics recommends that all Delaware LLCs engage qualified legal counsel to ensure compliance with the CTA, including the preparation and timely submission of initial BOI reports and any necessary amendments.
