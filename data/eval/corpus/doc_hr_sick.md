# Meridian Analytics — Sick Leave Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_sick
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-03-10

## Purpose

This policy outlines the sick leave provisions for all full-time
employees of Meridian Analytics, including the requirements for medical
certification and absence notification. The policy is designed to
support employees who are unable to work due to illness or injury while
ensuring appropriate documentation for extended absences.

## Scope and eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for paid sick leave from their
first day of employment. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated
entitlement based on their contracted hours. Contractors and interns
are not covered by this policy and should refer to their assignment
agreements for illness-related absence provisions.

## Entitlement and certification

Full-time employees accrue 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Employees may take up to **3 consecutive days** of sick leave without
submitting a medical certificate. If an absence extends beyond 3 days,
a medical certificate from a licensed practitioner must be provided
within 5 business days of returning to work. The certificate must
confirm that the employee was unfit for work during the absence period
and, where possible, indicate the expected duration of the condition.

## Notification procedure

Employees must notify their direct manager and log the absence in the
HR portal by 9:00 AM local time on the first day of illness. For planned
medical procedures, notice should be given at least 7 days in advance.
If the employee is unable to log the absence themselves due to the
nature of their illness, a manager or designated colleague may submit
the notification on their behalf.

## Relationship to other leave types

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and does not accrue from year
to year. Unused sick leave days expire at the end of each calendar year.
Employees who exhaust their 12 sick leave days may request to draw from
accrued annual leave or apply for short-term disability coverage through
Meridian's benefits provider. Annual leave used in place of sick leave
is deducted from the employee's annual leave entitlement and is subject
to the policies described in the Annual Leave Policy (doc_hr_leave).

## Exceptions and special circumstances

Employees with chronic or long-term medical conditions may request an
individual sick leave accommodation plan through People Operations.
Such plans may include adjusted attendance expectations, flexible
certification requirements, or referral to Meridian's Employee
Assistance Program for additional support.

## FAQ

**Q: What happens if I fall ill while on annual leave?**
A: If you can provide a medical certificate covering the illness period,
the days may be reclassified as sick leave and your annual leave days
will be restored.

**Q: Can sick leave be used to care for a dependent family member?**
A: No. Sick leave is intended for the employee's own illness or injury.
Employees requiring time off to care for a family member should refer
to the personal days provisions in the Paid Time Off Policy
(doc_hr_pto).
