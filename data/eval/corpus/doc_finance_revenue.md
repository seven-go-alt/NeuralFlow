# Meridian Analytics — Q3 2025 EMEA Revenue Summary

**Document ID:** doc_finance_revenue
**Owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** 2025-10-15

## Purpose

This document summarizes the total revenue recognized for the EMEA region in Q3 2025. Revenue figures are reported in accordance with ASC 606 and represent the aggregate of all subscription and services revenue generated from Meridian Analytics customers in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa.

## EMEA region Q3 2025 revenue

The total revenue for Q3 2025 in the EMEA region was $4,200,000. This includes revenue from all Meridian Analytics product lines sold to EMEA-based customers, including the Search Core platform, Embedding API, and the Context Engine. The Q3 2025 EMEA revenue represents a 22% increase over Q3 2024 and a 6% sequential increase over Q2 2025.

## Revenue breakdown by product line

EMEA region revenue for Q3 2025 was broken down as follows:

- **Search Core platform**: $2,100,000 (50% of total), driven by enterprise renewals and two new logos in the financial services sector
- **Embedding API**: $1,260,000 (30% of total), with strong adoption among AI-driven startups in the region
- **Context Engine**: $840,000 (20% of total), reflecting the continued ramp of the newer SaaS product launched in Q2 2025

## Revenue by customer segment

Within the EMEA region, revenue was distributed across customer segments as follows: enterprise customers (accounts with annual contract value over $100,000) contributed $2,940,000 or 70% of total EMEA revenue, mid-market customers contributed $840,000 or 20%, and small business customers contributed $420,000 or 10%. The enterprise segment continues to be the primary growth driver in the region.

## Regional performance notes

The United Kingdom and Germany together accounted for 55% of EMEA revenue in Q3 2025, with the UK at $1,260,000 and Germany at $1,050,000. France, the Nordics, and the Middle East contributed the remaining balance. The EMEA region remains Meridian's second-largest market by revenue after North America.

## Revision history

This summary was last updated on 15 October 2025. Final Q3 2025 revenue figures have been reviewed by the Finance team and the external auditors.
