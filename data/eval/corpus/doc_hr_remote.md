# Meridian Analytics — Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_remote
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-02-20

## Purpose

This policy governs remote work arrangements for employees of Meridian
Analytics, including guidelines for working from locations within the
same country and from a different country. Meridian supports flexible
working arrangements while ensuring business continuity, team
collaboration, and compliance with applicable laws.

## Scope and eligibility

All full-time employees in good standing are eligible to request remote
work arrangements. Employees must have completed their probation period
(typically 90 days) before applying for a fully remote arrangement.
Certain roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities
management and select laboratory-based positions, may not be eligible
for remote work and will be clearly identified in the job description.

## Standard remote work

Employees may work remotely from within their country of employment for
up to 3 days per week without prior approval, subject to team scheduling
needs. Fully remote arrangements require manager approval and a signed
remote work agreement that specifies expectations around availability,
communication, and performance measurement.

## International remote work

Employees are permitted to work remotely from a different country for
up to 30 days per calendar year without special international approval.
Working from a different country for **more than 30 consecutive days**
requires written approval from both the employee's director and the
People Operations team at least 4 weeks in advance. Approvals for
extended international remote work are considered on a case-by-case
basis and depend on the employee's role, time zone overlap requirements,
and applicable tax and employment law implications. Approved
international remote work arrangements are reviewed on a quarterly
basis and may be revoked if business needs change.

## Responsibilities

Remote employees must maintain a secure internet connection, comply with
Meridian's data security policies, and be available during core working
hours (9:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their registered time zone). Meridian
provides a home office stipend of $750 per year for remote employees.
The stipend is paid in quarterly installments and may be used for
ergonomic equipment, internet service upgrades, or office supplies.

## Equipment and IT support

Meridian provides remote employees with a company laptop, monitor,
headset, and necessary peripherals. IT support is available remotely
during standard business hours. Employees are responsible for
safeguarding company equipment and reporting loss or damage within
24 hours.

## Related policies

For leave arrangements while working remotely, refer to the Annual
Leave Policy (doc_hr_leave) and the Paid Time Off Policy (doc_hr_pto).
Data security requirements for remote access are detailed in the IT
Security Policy.

## Revision history

This policy was last updated on 20 February 2026 and will be reviewed
annually. Changes to international remote work provisions may be made
more frequently in response to regulatory updates in jurisdictions
where employees are located.
