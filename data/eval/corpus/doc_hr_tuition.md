# Meridian Analytics — Tuition Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_tuition
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

## Purpose

This policy defines the tuition reimbursement program at Meridian
Analytics, which supports employees in pursuing job-related education
and professional development. The program is designed to enhance
employee skills, support career growth, and strengthen Meridian's
talent base.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at
least 6 months of continuous service with Meridian Analytics.
Part-time employees working at least 30 hours per week are also
eligible on a pro-rated reimbursement basis. Courses and programs
must be job-related or part of a structured career development plan
approved by the employee's manager. Purely recreational or hobby
courses are not eligible.

## Maximum reimbursement amount

The maximum tuition reimbursement amount is **$5,250 per calendar
year** per employee. This amount aligns with the IRS limit for
tax-free educational assistance. Any reimbursement exceeding $5,250
in a given calendar year will be treated as taxable income to the
employee. The $5,250 limit applies to the total of all reimbursements
received in a single calendar year, regardless of when the course
was completed.

## Covered expenses

The following expenses are covered under the tuition reimbursement
program:

- Tuition and mandatory fees for accredited degree programs and
  certificate courses
- Required textbooks and course materials
- Registration fees for professional certifications relevant to the
  employee's role
- Laboratory and technology fees required by the educational
  institution

Expenses not covered include: travel costs, parking, meals,
entertainment, membership dues (unless specifically required for
certification), and late registration fees. Computer hardware and
software are not covered unless explicitly required by the course
and pre-approved in writing.

## Approval process

To qualify for tuition reimbursement, employees must:

1. Be a full-time employee with at least 6 months of continuous service
   at Meridian Analytics.
2. Receive prior written approval from their manager and the VP of
   their department before enrolling in the course or program.
3. Earn a grade of C or higher (or "Pass" in a pass/fail system) in
   the course.
4. Submit the reimbursement request through the HR portal within 30
   days of course completion, along with proof of payment and grade.

Approval requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the course
start date. Retroactive approval is not granted. Employees should
submit a Tuition Reimbursement Pre-Approval Form available in the HR
portal.

## Service commitment

Employees who receive more than $3,000 in tuition reimbursement in a
calendar year agree to remain employed at Meridian for 12 months
following the final reimbursement payment. If the employee resigns
voluntarily before the 12-month period ends, a pro-rated portion of
the reimbursement must be repaid. The repayment obligation does not
apply in the case of involuntary termination due to redundancy or
workforce reduction.

## Reimbursement timeline

Approved reimbursement requests are processed within 30 business days
of submission. Payments are made via the regular payroll system and
are included in the employee's next available pay cycle following
approval. Employees may track the status of their reimbursement
request in the HR portal.
