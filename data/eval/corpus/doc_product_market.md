**Document ID:** doc_product_market

## Overview

This document presents the market opportunity size for the healthcare vertical according to the 2025 annual report at Meridian Analytics. The analysis was conducted by the Product Management and Corporate Strategy teams as part of the annual strategic planning process, and the findings inform the product roadmap for FY 2026.

## Market opportunity size for the healthcare vertical

According to the 2025 annual report, the market opportunity size for the healthcare vertical is estimated at $1.4 billion in total addressable market (TAM) for AI-powered document analytics and retrieval solutions in healthcare. This represents the portion of the broader healthcare analytics market that aligns with Meridian's core capabilities in document ingestion, embedding, semantic search, and batch processing.

The market opportunity is segmented as follows:

| Sub-vertical | TAM | Serviceable addressable market (SAM) | Growth rate (CAGR) |
|-------------|-----|--------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Clinical document analysis | $480M | $165M | 28% |
| Medical research literature | $350M | $120M | 22% |
| Healthcare compliance and audit | $290M | $95M | 31% |
| Patient records management | $280M | $90M | 18% |

The healthcare vertical market opportunity size was identified as the highest-growth vertical in the 2025 annual report, with an average CAGR of 24.7% across all sub-segments. Meridian currently commands an estimated 3.2% market share in the healthcare vertical, representing $44.8 million in FY 2025 revenue. The 2025 annual report sets a target of increasing this share to 7.5% by the end of FY 2028.

## Competitive landscape

The 2025 annual report identifies the following key competitors in the healthcare vertical: HealthVector AI (22% market share), MediSearch Technologies (15%), and DocuAnalytics Health (11%). Meridian's competitive advantage lies in its multilingual embedding capabilities and the batch processing throughput, which are particularly well-suited for large-scale medical literature indexing and clinical trial document management.

## Go-to-market strategy for healthcare

Based on the market opportunity analysis in the 2025 annual report, Meridian will pursue the following go-to-market strategy for the healthcare vertical:

- Hire 8 dedicated healthcare sales representatives in FY 2026
- Achieve HIPAA compliance certification by Q3 2026 (audit currently in progress)
- Develop pre-built data connectors for major EMR systems (Epic, Cerner, Meditech)
- Establish a healthcare advisory board with 5 industry leaders
- Launch a vertical-specific pricing model with volume discounts for large hospital systems

## Revenue projections

The 2025 annual report projects that healthcare vertical revenue will grow from $44.8 million in FY 2025 to $72 million in FY 2026, representing a 60.7% year-over-year increase. By FY 2028, the healthcare vertical is expected to contribute $142 million in annual revenue, representing 18% of Meridian's total projected revenue.
