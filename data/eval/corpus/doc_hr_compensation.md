# Meridian Analytics — Compensation Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_compensation
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-01-20

## Purpose

This policy describes the compensation framework for all full-time
employees of Meridian Analytics, covering base salary, bonus
structures, and the calculation of total annual compensation. The
policy is designed to attract, retain, and motivate talent while
ensuring internal equity and market competitiveness.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Meridian Analytics
regardless of location, unless otherwise specified in an individual
employment contract. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated base
salary but remain eligible for bonus participation on the same terms
as full-time staff in equivalent roles.

## Base salary

Each position at Meridian has a defined salary band based on role,
experience level, and geographic location. Base salaries are reviewed
annually in April and adjusted based on market benchmarks and
individual performance. Employees who are promoted mid-year receive
a base salary adjustment effective from the first day of the month
following the promotion date.

## Bonus and annual compensation

Eligible employees receive a performance bonus calculated as a
percentage of their base salary. Bonus percentages vary by level:
individual contributors 0–10%, managers 10–15%, and directors 15–20%.
For example, an employee with a base salary of $80,000 and a bonus
percentage of 15% would receive a $12,000 bonus, bringing the total
annual compensation to $92,000. The actual bonus payout is also
adjusted by the employee's performance rating for the year, using
the rating factors defined in the Annual Performance Bonus Policy
(doc_hr_bonus).

## Total annual compensation definition

Total annual compensation is the sum of base salary plus any bonus
awarded during the calendar year. It excludes equity grants, benefits
contributions, and one-time awards such as signing or retention
bonuses. Employees can view their complete compensation breakdown in
the HR portal. Total annual compensation figures are used internally
for benchmarking and salary band calibration.

## Equity and long-term incentives

Meridian offers equity grants to eligible employees as a separate
component of total rewards. Equity grants are governed by the
Meridian Analytics Equity Incentive Plan and are not considered part
of total annual compensation for the purposes of this policy.

## Exceptions

Any exception to this compensation policy, including off-cycle salary
adjustments or special bonus arrangements, must be approved in writing
by the VP of People Operations and the Chief Financial Officer.

## Revision history

This policy was last updated on 20 January 2026. It supersedes all
previous compensation guidelines. The policy will be reviewed annually
in conjunction with the April salary review cycle.
