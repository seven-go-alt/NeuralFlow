# Accounting Memorandum: Lease Classification Under ASC 842

**Document ID:** doc_fin_lease_class
**Owner:** Office of the Corporate Controller, Meridian Analytics
**Last Updated:** March 28, 2026

## Overview of ASC 842 Lease Classification

ASC 842, Leases, requires lessees to classify leases as either finance leases or operating leases at the commencement date. This classification determines the pattern of expense recognition and the presentation of the lease on the balance sheet and income statement. Under ASC 842, a lease is classified as a finance lease if any one of five specified criteria is met. If none of the criteria are met, the lease is classified as an operating lease. This memorandum evaluates the classification of a lease when the lease term equals 80 percent of the asset's remaining economic life.

## The Five Finance Lease Classification Criteria

ASC 842-10-25-2 specifies five criteria for finance lease classification. The first is that the lease transfers ownership of the underlying asset to the lessee by the end of the lease term. The second is that the lease grants the lessee an option to purchase the asset that the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise. The third is that the lease term is for the major part of the remaining economic life of the asset. The fourth is that the present value of lease payments and any guaranteed residual value equals or exceeds substantially all of the fair value of the asset. The fifth is that the asset is so specialized that it has no alternative use to the lessor at lease end.

## The Major Part Threshold and Economic Life

The third criterion requires assessment of whether the lease term constitutes a major part of the remaining economic life of the underlying asset. ASC 842 removed the bright-line 75 percent threshold that existed under ASC 840, replacing it with the principle-based concept of a "major part" of the remaining economic life. However, the Basis for Conclusions accompanying ASC 842 acknowledges that entities may continue to apply the 75 percent threshold as a practical guideline for determining what constitutes a major part of the remaining economic life, provided it is applied consistently as a matter of accounting policy.

## Application to an 80 Percent Lease Term

When the lease term is 80 percent of the asset's remaining economic life, this exceeds both the historical 75 percent bright-line threshold under ASC 840 and the commonly applied guideline under ASC 842. Therefore, the third classification criterion is met and the lease must be classified as a finance lease under ASC 842. This conclusion holds regardless of whether the entity applies the 75 percent guideline or makes its own qualitative assessment of what constitutes a major part of the economic life.

## Impact on Financial Reporting

Under a finance lease, the lessee recognizes a right-of-use asset and a lease liability at the present value of the lease payments. The lessee amortizes the right-of-use asset over the lease term and recognizes interest expense on the liability using the effective interest method. The total expense recognized is generally higher in the early years compared to an operating lease.
