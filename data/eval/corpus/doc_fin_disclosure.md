# Disclosure Requirements for Related Party Transactions in the Annual 10-K Filing

**Document ID:** MER-FIN-2026-005
**Owner:** SEC Reporting and Compliance Group
**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

## Applicable Guidance

This memorandum summarizes the disclosure requirements for related party transactions in Meridian Analytics' annual 10-K filing, as mandated by SEC Regulation S-X and ASC 850 (Related Party Disclosures). Meridian Analytics, as a privately held company, files voluntarily with the SEC in connection with its public debt offerings and is therefore subject to Regulation S-X.

## Definition of Related Party Transactions

Under SEC rules and ASC 850, related party transactions include any business deal or arrangement between Meridian Analytics and any of the following related parties:

- Directors and executive officers of the company
- Immediate family members of directors and executive officers
- Beneficial owners of more than 5% of the company's equity
- Affiliates and entities under common control
- Any other party that can exert significant influence over management or operating policies

Transactions subject to disclosure include sales, purchases, loans, guarantees, service arrangements, and any other transfers of assets or liabilities.

## Required Disclosures

Under SEC Regulation S-X, the 10-K must disclose the following information for each material related party transaction:

- **Nature of the relationship:** A clear description of how the counterparty is related to Meridian Analytics, including the specific basis for the related party classification.
- **Description of the transaction:** A narrative description of the transaction's purpose, terms, and business rationale, including whether the terms are comparable to those that would be obtained in an arm's-length transaction.
- **Dollar amounts of transactions:** The aggregate dollar amount of transactions with each related party for each period presented in the financial statements.
- **Terms and conditions:** Specific payment terms, interest rates, maturity dates, collateral arrangements, and any guarantees.
- **Amounts due to or from related parties:** The outstanding balance of amounts owed by or to related parties, including the nature and terms of any unsettled obligations.
- **Any provisions for doubtful accounts:** If Meridian has recorded an allowance for amounts due from related parties, the amount and basis for the estimate must be disclosed.
- **Any subsequent changes:** Material changes in the related party relationship or transactions that occurred after the balance sheet date.

## Materiality Assessment

Meridian evaluates related party transactions for disclosure purposes using both quantitative and qualitative materiality thresholds. Transactions exceeding $120,000 are presumed material under SEC rules and require disclosure unless determined to be immaterial after qualitative analysis. Transactions below this threshold are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

## Internal Review Process

All potential related party transactions are identified through annual related party questionnaires distributed to directors, executive officers, and significant shareholders. The SEC Reporting Group reviews all responses and compiles the disclosure for inclusion in the 10-K. The Audit Committee of the Board of Directors reviews and approves all material related party transactions.
