# Meridian Analytics — Annual Leave Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_leave
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-01-15

## Purpose

This policy defines the annual leave entitlement for all full-time
employees of Meridian Analytics. Annual leave is intended for
rest, recreation, and personal pursuits and must be approved in
advance through the standard request process.

## Scope and eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for annual leave from their
first day of employment. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated
entitlement calculated as a percentage of the full-time allocation
based on their contracted hours. Temporary and contract staff are
not covered by this policy and should refer to their individual
employment agreements.

## Entitlement

| Years of service | Annual leave entitlement |
|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 working days |
| 3–5 years | 20 working days |
| More than 5 years | 25 working days (maximum) |

The maximum annual leave entitlement is **25 working days** per calendar
year, granted to employees with more than 5 years of service. Service
is calculated from the employee's most recent start date at Meridian
Analytics and is measured in complete years.

## Accrual and carry-over

Leave accrues monthly at 1/12 of the annual entitlement. Up to 5 unused
days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year;
carried-over days expire on 31 March. Employees who do not use their
carried-over days by this date forfeit them automatically. Requests for
carry-over beyond 5 days require VP of People Operations approval and
are granted only in exceptional business circumstances.

## How to request leave

Submit requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.
Requests longer than 10 consecutive days require director approval.
Public holidays observed by the employee's registered office do not
count against the entitlement. Employees should ensure adequate
coverage for their responsibilities during the leave period and
discuss timing with their manager before submitting.

## Exceptions

In exceptional circumstances, such as serious illness of a family
member or unforeseen personal hardship, employees may request
advance payment of accrued but not yet vested annual leave. Such
requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by People Operations.
