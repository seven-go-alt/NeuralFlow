# Meridian Analytics — Annual Performance Bonus Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_bonus
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-01-25

## Purpose

This policy describes the annual performance bonus program at Meridian
Analytics, including the eligible departments, calculation methodology,
and payout schedule. This policy was updated as part of the broader
compensation policy revision in January 2026, which aligned bonus
eligibility with the company's strategic objectives for the upcoming
fiscal year.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at
least 90 days of continuous service by 31 December of the performance
year. Employees hired after 1 October of the performance year are not
eligible for that year's bonus but will be considered in the next
annual cycle. Part-time employees eligible under this policy receive a
pro-rated bonus based on their FTE percentage.

## Eligible departments

Based on the compensation policy updated in January 2026, the following
departments are eligible for the annual performance bonus:

- Engineering
- Sales
- Marketing
- Product
- Customer Success
- Data Science
- Finance
- People Operations

Departments not eligible for the performance bonus include Legal and
Administrative roles; these teams receive alternative compensation
structures reviewed separately. The list of eligible departments is
reviewed annually and may be adjusted based on business needs.

## Bonus calculation

The performance bonus for eligible departments is calculated as a
percentage of the employee's base salary, determined by level:

- Individual contributors: up to 10%
- Managers: up to 15%
- Directors and above: up to 20%

The final bonus percentage is multiplied by the employee's performance
rating factor (ranging from 0.8 for "meets expectations" to 1.5 for
"exceptional performance"). Employees rated below "meets expectations"
are not eligible for a bonus for that performance year. The performance
rating factor is determined through the annual performance review
process conducted in January.

## Payout schedule

Performance bonuses are paid annually in February, based on the
prior calendar year's performance review results. Employees must be
actively employed at Meridian on the payout date to receive the bonus,
unless otherwise specified in their employment agreement. Bonuses are
paid via the regular payroll system and are subject to applicable tax
withholdings.

## Discretionary adjustments

The VP of People Operations, in consultation with department heads,
reserves the right to make discretionary adjustments to individual
bonus amounts based on extraordinary contributions or market
conditions. Such adjustments are documented in writing and approved
by the Chief Financial Officer.

## FAQ

**Q: If I transfer departments mid-year, which bonus eligibility
applies?**
A: The bonus eligibility is determined by the department you are in
on 31 December of the performance year. If you transfer to a
non-eligible department before that date, you will not receive a
performance bonus for that year.

**Q: Are signing bonuses covered by this policy?**
A: No. Signing bonuses and retention bonuses are one-time awards
governed by separate agreements and are not part of the annual
performance bonus program.
