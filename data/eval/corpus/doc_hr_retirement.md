# Meridian Analytics — 401k Retirement Plan

**Document ID:** doc_hr_retirement
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-05-15

## Purpose

This policy describes the 401k retirement savings plan offered by
Meridian Analytics, including the company matching contribution and
the vesting schedule for employer contributions. The plan is designed
to help employees build long-term financial security for retirement.

## Plan eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible to participate in the 401(k)
plan after 90 days of continuous employment. Employees may contribute
up to the annual IRS limit through pre-tax or Roth deferrals, with
the ability to change contribution percentages at any time.
Employees who do not actively enroll after 90 days are automatically
enrolled at a 3% pre-tax contribution rate, with the right to opt
out or adjust their contribution percentage at any time.

## Company matching contribution

Meridian provides a matching contribution of 50% on the first 6% of
an employee's eligible compensation that they defer into the plan.
For example, if an employee earning $80,000 defers 6% ($4,800), the
company matching contribution would be $2,400 (50% of $4,800). The
match is calculated and deposited each pay period. There is no
maximum dollar cap on the matching contribution beyond the percentage
formula described above. Catch-up contributions for employees aged 50
and over are not matched.

## Vesting schedule

Employee contributions are always 100% vested. Employer matching
contributions follow a graded vesting schedule:

| Years of service | Vested percentage |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | 0% |
| 1 year | 25% |
| 2 years | 50% |
| 3 years | 75% |
| 4 or more years | 100% |

Matching contributions become fully vested after 4 years of service.
If an employee leaves Meridian before reaching full vesting, only the
vested portion of employer contributions is retained. Years of service
are calculated from the employee's most recent hire date and include
all periods of continuous full-time employment.

## Investment options

Participants may choose from a range of investment options offered
through the plan's recordkeeper, including target-date funds, index
funds, and actively managed options. Employees who do not make an
investment election will have their contributions defaulted into the
appropriate target-date fund based on their expected retirement year.

## Loans and withdrawals

Participants may borrow against their 401(k) balance up to the lesser
of $50,000 or 50% of the vested account value. Hardship withdrawals
are available under IRS guidelines. Loan repayments are made through
payroll deduction. Defaulting on a 401(k) loan is treated as a
deemed distribution and is subject to income tax and applicable
penalties.
