# Meridian Analytics — Personal Device (BYOD) Security Policy

**Document ID:** doc_it_security
**Owner:** IT Security
**Last updated:** 2026-02-10

## Purpose

This document defines the company policy on using personal devices for work purposes at Meridian Analytics. As a data analytics SaaS company, Meridian must protect client data and internal systems. The Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) policy establishes the security requirements, approved device types, and enforcement mechanisms that govern the use of personally owned smartphones, tablets, and laptops for accessing company resources.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and interns who wish to use their personal devices for work-related activities. Participation in the BYOD program is voluntary. Employees who opt out will be issued a company-owned device. Personal devices used under this policy must meet the minimum security requirements defined below and be enrolled in Meridian's Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform within 5 business days of requesting BYOD access.

## Security requirements

All personal devices used for work must comply with the following mandatory security measures:

- **Device encryption:** Full-disk encryption must be enabled and verified by the MDM agent.
- **Screen lock:** A strong passcode (minimum 8 characters) or biometric authentication must be active, with auto-lock set to 5 minutes or less.
- **Operating system updates:** The device must run a currently supported OS version. Security patches must be installed within 14 days of release.
- **Prohibited configurations:** Rooted or jailbroken devices are strictly prohibited from accessing any Meridian systems. Devices that are found to be compromised will be remotely wiped.
- **Network security:** Personal devices must use a VPN when connecting to Meridian's internal network over public Wi-Fi.

## Approved uses and restrictions

Personal devices enrolled in the BYOD program may be used to access Meridian email, calendar, Slack, and approved SaaS applications. The following restrictions apply:

- Company data may not be stored on local device storage outside of MDM-managed containers.
- Copying company data to personal cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal cloud storage services) is prohibited.
- If a personal device is lost or stolen, the employee must report the incident to IT Security within 2 hours. Meridian reserves the right to remotely wipe company data from the device.
- Upon termination of employment, all company data must be removed from the personal device under IT supervision.

## Enforcement and non-compliance

Non-compliance with this security policy may result in revocation of BYOD privileges and, in cases of data exposure, disciplinary action up to and including termination. IT Security conducts quarterly audits of enrolled devices. Devices that fail compliance checks are quarantined, and the employee receives a notification with remediation steps.
