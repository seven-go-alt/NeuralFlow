# Meridian Analytics — Health Insurance Benefits (Dental and Vision)

**Document ID:** doc_hr_benefits
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-01-10

## Purpose

This document describes the dental and vision insurance benefits
available to full-time employees of Meridian Analytics, including
coverage details, out-of-network reimbursement, and annual maximum
limits. These benefits are part of Meridian's comprehensive total
rewards package designed to support employee health and wellness.

## Scope and eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible to enroll in dental and vision
benefits from their date of hire. Benefits coverage begins on the
employee's first day of employment with Meridian Analytics.
Part-time employees who work at least 30 hours per week are also
eligible. Dependents, including a spouse or domestic partner and
children up to age 26, may be added to both plans.

## Dental coverage

Meridian offers two dental insurance plan options through the company's
benefits provider:

- **Basic Plan:** Covers 100% of preventive care (cleanings, exams,
  X-rays), 80% of basic procedures (fillings, extractions), and 50%
  of major procedures (crowns, bridges, root canals). The annual
  maximum is $1,500 per person.
- **Premium Plan:** Same preventive and basic coverage as the Basic
  Plan, plus 80% of major procedures. The annual maximum is $2,500
  per person.

**Out-of-network coverage** for dental care is reimbursed at 60% of
the usual and customary fee for both plans. The out-of-network annual
maximum is $1,000 per person under the Basic Plan and $1,500 under
the Premium Plan. Employees who choose out-of-network providers should
confirm that the provider accepts assignment of benefits to avoid
upfront payment of the full fee.

## Vision coverage

Vision benefits include an annual eye exam covered at 100%, an
allowance of $150 toward frames every 24 months, and a $130 allowance
for contact lenses. The vision plan also covers one comprehensive eye
exam per calendar year. Contact lens fittings and elective refractive
surgery are not covered under the standard vision plan.

**Out-of-network coverage** for vision services is reimbursed at 70%
of the usual and customary fee, up to the same annual maximum of
$150 for frames and $130 for contact lenses. The plan also covers
one eye exam per calendar year at 80% reimbursement for out-of-network
providers. Out-of-network claims must be submitted by the employee
using the vision claim form available in the HR portal.

## Enrollment

New employees may enroll in dental and vision benefits within 30 days
of their start date. Open enrollment occurs annually in November, with
changes taking effect on 1 January of the following year. Mid-year
changes are permitted only in the event of a qualifying life event
such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of other coverage.

## Coordination with medical insurance

Dental and vision benefits are administered separately from Meridian's
medical health insurance plan. Employees may enroll in dental and
vision benefits independently of their medical plan election. Premiums
for dental and vision coverage are deducted pre-tax through the
company's payroll system.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I change my dental plan from Basic to Premium outside of
open enrollment?**
A: Only during open enrollment in November or within 30 days of a
qualifying life event.

**Q: Is orthodontic coverage included in either dental plan?**
A: No. Orthodontic treatment, including braces and aligners, is not
covered under either the Basic or Premium dental plan.
