# Meridian Analytics — Resignation and Notice Period Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_notice
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-02-01

## Purpose

This policy specifies the notice period requirements for employees
of Meridian Analytics who voluntarily resign from their position,
with special provisions for director level and above. The policy
ensures orderly transitions and business continuity while respecting
employees' right to pursue new opportunities.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of
Meridian Analytics who choose to resign voluntarily. Employees on
fixed-term contracts should refer to the terms specified in their
individual employment agreements. Involuntary terminations and
redundancies are governed by separate policies and are not covered
here.

## Standard notice period

All employees are required to provide a written notice of resignation
to their direct manager and the People Operations team. The standard
notice period is 30 calendar days for individual contributors and
team leads. The notice period begins on the date the resignation
letter is received by People Operations.

## Director level and above

Employees at the **director level** and above must provide a minimum
notice period of 60 calendar days upon resignation. This extended
period allows for a thorough transition of leadership responsibilities,
client relationships, and strategic initiatives. The 60-day notice
requirement applies to Directors, Senior Directors, Vice Presidents,
and all C-suite roles. The company may, at its discretion, negotiate
a shorter notice period for senior roles if a suitable transition plan
can be executed more quickly.

## Resignation procedure

The resignation process is as follows:

1. Submit a formal resignation letter via the HR portal.
2. Schedule an exit interview with People Operations.
3. Work with your manager to create a transition plan during the
   notice period.
4. Return all company property on or before the last working day.

Exit interviews are conducted to gather feedback and ensure a smooth
handover of responsibilities. Participation in the exit interview is
expected but voluntary.

## Garden leave and payment in lieu

Meridian reserves the right to place resigning employees on garden
leave for all or part of the notice period. Alternatively, the company
may make a payment in lieu of notice equal to the base salary that
would have been earned during the required notice period. Garden leave
arrangements are at the company's discretion and are typically used
when the employee has access to sensitive business information or
client relationships.

## Exceptions

In rare circumstances, such as an employee being recalled from a
long-term international assignment, the notice period may be adjusted
by mutual agreement between the employee and Meridian. Any deviation
from the standard notice period must be documented in writing and
approved by the VP of People Operations.
