# Meridian Analytics — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_pto
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This policy describes the consolidated Paid Time Off (PTO) program at
Meridian Analytics, under which vacation, sick leave, and personal
days are managed through a single PTO bank. The PTO program provides
employees with flexibility to manage their time off across different
needs while simplifying tracking and administration.

## Scope and eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for the PTO program from their
first day of employment. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated PTO
bank calculated as a percentage of the full-time allocation based on
their contracted weekly hours. Temporary employees and contractors
are not eligible and should refer to their individual agreements.

## PTO bank structure

All full-time employees receive a combined PTO bank of 25 days per
calendar year. The PTO bank covers vacation, sick leave, and personal
days collectively. Employees may allocate their PTO bank days across
these categories as needed, subject to the approval processes outlined
below. **Personal days count toward the PTO bank** and must be tracked
through the same system as vacation and sick leave. Employees should
monitor their remaining balance in the HR portal to avoid exceeding
their annual allocation.

## Usage by category

- **Vacation:** Used for planned time off, holidays, and travel. Must
  be requested at least 10 working days in advance. Vacation requests
  exceeding 5 consecutive days require manager approval.
- **Sick leave:** Used for illness or medical appointments. Notification
  must be given by 9:00 AM on the day of absence. Additional
  certification requirements are described in the Sick Leave Policy
  (doc_hr_sick).
- **Personal days:** Used for any other personal reasons not covered by
  vacation or sick leave, such as family events, appointments, or
  errands. Personal days require manager approval and are drawn from
  the same PTO bank. Personal days should be requested at least 2
  working days in advance unless the need is urgent.

## Example

If an employee uses 10 days of vacation, 5 days of sick leave, and
3 personal days in a calendar year, the total PTO days used from the
PTO bank would be 18 days (10 + 5 + 3). The remaining balance in the
PTO bank would be 7 days. This example illustrates how the PTO bank
functions as a single pool from which all three categories are drawn.

## Carry-over and payout

Up to 5 unused PTO bank days may be carried over to the following
calendar year. Accrued but unused days beyond the carry-over limit
are forfeited at year-end. Upon termination of employment, any
accrued but untaken PTO days will be paid out in the employee's
final paycheck, up to a maximum of 10 days. No payout is made for
PTO days in excess of 10 at the time of termination.

## Approval and scheduling

Managers should review PTO requests to ensure adequate team coverage.
Peak periods, such as end-of-quarter and end-of-year, may have
restrictions on concurrent PTO within a team. Employees are encouraged
to coordinate with their teammates before submitting requests during
these periods.
