# Meridian Analytics — Travel Expense Reporting Policy

**Document ID:** doc_finance_expense
**Owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** 2026-02-01

## Purpose

This policy defines the procedures for submitting travel expense reports at Meridian Analytics, including the specific approval workflow required for expenses exceeding $5,000. All employees who incur travel-related expenses on behalf of the company must comply with this policy.

## Scope and applicability

This policy applies to all Meridian Analytics employees, contractors, and consultants who incur travel expenses for business purposes. Travel expenses include airfare, lodging, meals, ground transportation, and other incidental expenses incurred during authorized business travel. All expense reports must be submitted through the company's expense management system within 30 days of the trip completion date.

## Standard expense reporting procedure

For all travel expenses, employees must itemize each expense with the date, amount, business purpose, and a receipt. Receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more. Standard expense reports are reviewed by the employee's direct manager and processed by the Finance team. Reimbursement is issued via direct deposit within 10 business days of approval.

## Procedure for expense reports over $5,000

When a travel expense report totals more than $5,000, the following additional requirements apply:

1. **Advance approval**: The traveler must obtain written approval from their department head and the Chief Financial Officer before incurring expenses that will result in a report exceeding $5,000. This advance approval must be attached to the expense report at the time of submission.

2. **Detailed supporting documentation**: In addition to standard receipts, the traveler must provide a detailed business justification explaining the necessity of the expenditures. For each line item over $1,000, a separate explanation of business purpose is required.

3. **Manager approval**: The expense report must be approved by the traveler's direct manager first, followed by the department head, and finally the Finance team. Approval is sequential and each approver must confirm that the expenses were business-necessary and within policy limits.

4. **CFO review**: The CFO or their designee reviews all expense reports over $5,000 as part of the final approval stage. The CFO may request additional documentation or justification before approving the report.

## Non-compliance consequences

Failure to obtain the required approvals for a travel expense report over $5,000 may result in delayed reimbursement or denial of the expense. Repeated violations may lead to disciplinary action, including revocation of travel privileges. Employees who submit expense reports without proper documentation will be asked to resubmit with the missing information.

## Revision history

This policy was last updated on 1 February 2026. It supersedes all previous travel expense reporting policies and will be reviewed annually.
