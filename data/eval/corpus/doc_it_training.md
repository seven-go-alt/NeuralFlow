# Meridian Analytics — IT Training and Certification Policy

**Document ID:** doc_it_training
**Owner:** IT Security
**Last updated:** 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This document defines the training certification requirements that employees must complete before gaining access to sensitive systems at Meridian Analytics. The policy ensures that all personnel handling production infrastructure, customer data, or internal financial systems possess the necessary knowledge to operate securely and in compliance with regulatory obligations.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party consultants who require access to production databases, customer data repositories, financial systems, or any system classified as Critical or High under Meridian's data classification framework. Role-specific training must be completed before access is provisioned, and refresher training is required annually.

## Training certification required before production database access

Before accessing the production database, every employee must complete the following training certification:

- **Meridian Data Security and Access Control Certification (MDS-101):** This certification covers database authentication protocols, least-privilege principles, data handling procedures, and incident reporting. The course takes approximately 4 hours to complete and includes a final assessment with a passing score of 85%. Employees who fail the assessment must wait 24 hours before retaking it.

The training certification is delivered through Meridian's internal learning management system (LMS). Upon successful completion, the LMS automatically updates the employee's access profile and triggers a notification to the IT Operations team, which then provisions the database credentials. The certification is valid for 12 months from the date of completion. Renewal requires completing the MDS-101 refresher module, which takes approximately 2 hours.

## Additional role-based certifications

Depending on the employee's role, additional training certifications may be required before accessing specific systems:

- **Cloud Infrastructure Certification (CLD-201):** Required for engineers granted access to public cloud production environments. Covers IAM policies, encryption at rest and in transit, and cloud security monitoring.
- **Customer Data Handling Certification (CDH-101):** Required for Customer Success and Support team members who access customer data for troubleshooting. Covers data minimization, deletion procedures, and privacy requirements.
- **Financial Systems Access Certification (FIN-101):** Required for Finance team members accessing the ERP and payroll systems. Covers segregation of duties and audit trail requirements.

## Certification maintenance

Employees must maintain their training certifications by completing annual refresher modules. The LMS sends automated reminders 60 and 30 days before expiration. If a certification expires, system access is automatically revoked until the employee completes the refresher module. Managers receive a notification when a team member's certification is overdue by more than 14 days.

## Exceptions

Managers may request a temporary exception to the training certification requirement for urgent access needs. Temporary exceptions are valid for a maximum of 5 business days and require written approval from the IT Security Director. The employee must complete the required training certification within the exception period.
