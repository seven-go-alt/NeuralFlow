# Meridian Analytics — Employee Expense Reimbursement SLA Policy

**Document ID:** doc_finance_sla
**Owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** 2026-01-10

## Purpose

This policy defines the processing time SLA for employee expense reimbursements at Meridian Analytics. The policy ensures that employees are reimbursed promptly for approved business expenses while maintaining adequate controls for review and approval.

## Standard processing time SLA

The standard processing time SLA for employee expense reimbursements is 10 business days from the date of submission. The SLA clock starts when the employee submits a complete expense report through the company's expense management system with all required receipts and business justifications attached. The processing time includes the manager approval step, the Finance review step, and the direct deposit initiation step.

## Processing timeline

The end-to-end reimbursement process follows this timeline:

1. **Submission**: Employee submits a complete expense report with itemized expenses and attached receipts.
2. **Manager approval**: The employee's direct manager reviews and approves the report within 3 business days of submission. If the manager has questions, the SLA is paused until the employee responds.
3. **Finance review**: The Finance team reviews the approved report within 5 business days of manager approval. Any discrepancies are communicated to the employee for resolution.
4. **Reimbursement**: Once Finance approves, reimbursement is initiated via direct deposit and typically arrives in the employee's bank account within 2 business days.

The total processing time SLA of 10 business days covers the entire end-to-end process from submission to reimbursement initiation.

## Priority reimbursement SLA

For urgent business travel or other exceptional circumstances, employees may request priority reimbursement processing. Priority requests must include a written justification from the employee's supervisor explaining why standard processing timing is insufficient. If approved, the priority SLA is 3 business days from the date of submission. Priority requests are subject to the same documentation requirements as standard expense reports.

## SLA exceptions

The processing time SLA may be extended under the following circumstances: incomplete documentation or missing receipts, expense reports that exceed $5,000 requiring CFO approval, expense reports flagged for audit review, or system outages affecting the expense management platform. In each case, the employee is notified of the expected delay and the reason for the exception.

## Compliance and monitoring

Finance tracks SLA compliance monthly and reports metrics to the CFO. The target is to meet the 10-business-day SLA for at least 95% of expense reports. Any sustained failure to meet this target triggers a process review to identify bottlenecks and implement improvements.
