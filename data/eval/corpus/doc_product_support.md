# Meridian Analytics — Premium Support Plan Total Cost of Ownership

**Document ID:** doc_product_support
**Owner:** Customer Success
**Last updated:** 2026-02-28

## Overview

This document provides the total cost of ownership calculation for the premium support plan over a 3-year period. The premium support plan offers dedicated support engineers, 1-hour response SLA, 24/7 coverage, and quarterly business reviews. The calculation assumes an annual fee of $12,000 with a 5% annual increase as specified in the standard premium support contract terms.

## Total cost of ownership calculation

The total cost of ownership for the premium support plan over a 3-year period is calculated by summing the annual fees across the three years, accounting for the 5% annual increase applied at the start of each subsequent contract year.

Given an annual fee of $12,000 with a 5% annual increase, the calculation is as follows:

- **Year 1:** $12,000.00 (initial annual fee)
- **Year 2:** $12,000.00 \* 1.05 = $12,600.00
- **Year 3:** $12,600.00 \* 1.05 = $13,230.00
- **Total cost of ownership over 3 years:** $12,000.00 + $12,600.00 + $13,230.00 = **$37,830.00**

The total cost of ownership for the premium support plan over a 3-year period is $37,830.00, given an annual fee of $12,000 with a 5% annual increase. This represents a 5.08% effective annual increase over the 3-year term.

## Premium support plan features

For the total cost of ownership of $37,830.00 over 3 years, customers receive the following premium support services:

- **24/7 support coverage** with a 1-hour response SLA for Critical severity issues
- **Dedicated support engineer** assigned to the customer account
- **Quarterly business reviews** with the Customer Success team
- **Access to the premium support portal** with ticket tracking and analytics
- **Proactive monitoring** of API usage, rate limiting, and error rates
- **Priority queue** for feature requests and bug fixes
- **Support for up to 5 designated contacts** per account

## Comparison with standard support

The standard support plan has an annual fee of $4,800 with a 3% annual increase. Over 3 years, the total cost of ownership for standard support is $4,800 + $4,944 + $5,092.32 = $14,836.32. The standard plan offers 8-hour response SLA, email-only support, and access to the knowledge base but does not include a dedicated engineer or quarterly reviews.

## Return on investment considerations

When evaluating the total cost of ownership for the premium support plan, customers should consider the potential cost of downtime. At the enterprise tier pricing of $1,000 per month for the Meridian Search Core product, a 4-hour outage could result in approximately $5,480 in lost revenue for a customer processing 500,000 API requests per month. The premium support plan's 1-hour response SLA reduces the mean time to resolution, directly mitigating this risk.

## How to purchase

Customers can add the premium support plan to any enterprise tier subscription. The plan is billed annually in advance. Multi-year contracts are available with a 10% discount on the total cost of ownership when committing to a 3-year term upfront, bringing the total to $34,047.00.
