# Meridian Analytics — Financial Audit Records Retention Policy

**Document ID:** doc_compliance_audit
**Owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** 2026-01-15

## Purpose

This document defines the retention period for financial audit records according to Meridian Analytics company policy. Proper retention of financial records is essential for regulatory compliance, tax obligations, and the successful completion of annual external audits. This policy applies to all financial records generated by Meridian's operations.

## Scope

This policy covers all financial audit records maintained by Meridian Analytics, including general ledger entries, accounts payable and receivable records, payroll records, tax filings, revenue recognition documentation, and any supporting schedules produced during internal or external audit activities. The policy applies to both electronic records stored in the company's financial systems and physical records retained at Meridian's headquarters.

## Retention period for financial audit records

According to company policy, the retention period for financial audit records is seven years from the end of the fiscal year in which the records were created or the audit was completed, whichever is later. This retention period is based on the standard statute of limitations for tax audits and financial reporting compliance requirements under applicable regulatory frameworks.

The following table summarizes the specific retention periods by record category:

| Record category | Retention period | Disposal method |
|-----------------|------------------|-----------------|
| General ledger and journals | 7 years | Secure shredding or cryptographic erasure |
| Accounts payable/receivable | 7 years | Secure shredding or cryptographic erasure |
| Payroll records | 7 years | Secure shredding or cryptographic erasure |
| Tax filings and supporting docs | 10 years | Permanent archival |
| Revenue recognition schedules | 7 years | Secure shredding or cryptographic erasure |
| Internal audit workpapers | 7 years | Secure shredding or cryptographic erasure |
| External audit reports | Permanent | Digital archival |

Records that fall under litigation hold must be preserved beyond the standard retention period until the hold is formally released by Legal. The Legal team notifies Finance when a litigation hold is active and provides the list of affected records.

## Record management procedures

Finance is responsible for ensuring that financial audit records are stored in a manner that preserves their integrity and prevents unauthorized modification. All electronic records are stored in Meridian's enterprise financial system with immutable audit logging enabled. Physical records are stored in a locked, access-controlled filing room at headquarters.

Quarterly, the Finance team reviews the record inventory and identifies records that have reached the end of their retention period. Records scheduled for disposal are reviewed by the Senior Accountant and approved by the Controller before deletion or shredding. A certificate of destruction is generated for each disposal batch and retained in the audit records archive.

## Exceptions

If a regulatory body or external auditor requests that records be retained beyond the standard retention period, Finance will comply with the request and document the extended retention schedule. Tax-related records may require a 10-year retention period in certain jurisdictions, as noted in the table above.
