# Revenue Recognition for Gift Card Breakage Under ASC 606

**Document ID:** MER-FIN-2026-003
**Owner:** Revenue Recognition Advisory Team
**Last Updated:** July 14, 2026

## Scope

This memorandum addresses the appropriate revenue recognition treatment for gift card breakage at Meridian Analytics' restaurant operator subsidiary. Breakage refers to the portion of gift card balances that management expects will never be redeemed by the customer. The guidance follows ASC 606-10-55-44 through 55-46, which provides specific guidance on breakage for prepaid cards.

## Background

The restaurant operator sells gift cards to customers for future dining services. Historical redemption patterns indicate that approximately 8% of gift card balances are never redeemed (the breakage rate). Management has sufficient historical data spanning five years to support a reliable estimate of expected breakage.

Under ASC 606, gift cards represent a contract liability (deferred revenue) because the entity has received consideration from the customer but has not yet satisfied its performance obligation. The key accounting question is when and how to recognize the unredeemed portion as revenue.

## Revenue Recognition Treatment

Meridian Analytics recognizes gift card breakage **proportionally as redemptions occur**, based on the expected breakage rate. This approach is consistent with the requirements of ASC 606-10-55-44.

Under this method, when a customer redeems a gift card, Meridian recognizes revenue equal to the amount redeemed plus a proportionate share of the expected breakage. The proportionate share is calculated as the expected breakage multiplied by the ratio of current period redemptions to total expected redemptions.

## Journal Entry Illustration

When a customer redeems a $100 gift card and the expected breakage rate is 8%:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Contract Liability (Deferred Revenue) | $100.00 | |
| Revenue | | $92.00 |
| Breakage Income | | $8.00 |

The breakage component is recognized as revenue in the same proportion as actual redemptions, ensuring that the revenue recognition pattern reflects the actual consumption of goods or services.

## Disclosure Requirements

Under ASC 606, Meridian must disclose the following related to gift card breakage:

- The nature of the gift card program and the associated breakage estimate
- The methodology used to estimate breakage rates
- The amount of breakage recognized in each reporting period
- The total contract liability balance related to outstanding gift cards

## Conclusion

Meridian Analytics recognizes breakage on gift cards proportionally as redemptions occur, based on an expected breakage rate derived from historical data. This treatment under ASC 606 ensures that revenue is recognized in a pattern that reflects the transfer of control to the customer.
