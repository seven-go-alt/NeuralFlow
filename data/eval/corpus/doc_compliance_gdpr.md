# Meridian Analytics — GDPR Compliance Checklist

**Document ID:** doc_compliance_gdpr
**Owner:** Legal and Compliance
**Last updated:** 2026-02-20

## Purpose

This document outlines the GDPR compliance checklist that Meridian Analytics follows to ensure lawful processing of personal data of individuals residing in the European Economic Area (EEA). The checklist covers data subject rights, lawful bases for processing, data protection impact assessments, and the requirement for data processing agreements with all third-party vendors that handle Meridian customer data.

## Scope

This checklist applies to all Meridian Analytics departments, systems, and processes that involve the collection, storage, or processing of personal data of EEA data subjects. It covers both customer data processed through the Meridian SaaS platform and employee data managed by People Operations. All third-party vendors that access or process this data are subject to the same requirements.

## Data processing agreements with third-party vendors

Yes, the GDPR compliance checklist requires data processing agreements with all third-party vendors who process personal data on behalf of Meridian Analytics. Every vendor that falls under this category must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that includes:

- A clear description of the nature, purpose, and duration of the data processing
- The categories of personal data and data subjects involved
- The vendor's obligation to process data only on Meridian's documented instructions
- Confidentiality commitments for personnel authorized to process the data
- Security measures the vendor implements, including encryption, access controls, and breach notification procedures
- Provisions for sub-processing, requiring prior written authorization
- Data deletion or return obligations upon termination of services
- An obligation to assist Meridian in responding to data subject access requests

The Legal and Compliance team maintains a vendor registry that tracks DPA status. New vendors must have an executed DPA before any data processing begins. The current registry contains 47 active third-party vendors with signed DPAs.

## GDPR compliance checklist items

The following items represent the core GDPR compliance checklist maintained by the Legal and Compliance team:

1. **Lawful basis documentation:** Each processing activity must have a documented lawful basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, or legitimate interests).
2. **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA):** A DPIA must be completed for any processing that is likely to result in high risk to data subjects. Meridian completed 12 DPIAs in the last fiscal year.
3. **Data Processing Agreements:** Executed DPAs must be on file for all third-party vendors. Currently 47 active DPAs are in place.
4. **Data subject request procedures:** Procedures must be in place to handle access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection requests within the 30-day statutory timeframe.
5. **Data breach notification:** Procedures must ensure notification to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach.
6. **Records of processing activities:** A register of processing activities must be maintained and updated quarterly.
7. **International transfer safeguards:** Standard Contractual Clauses or Binding Corporate Rules must be in place for any transfer of personal data outside the EEA.
8. **Data Protection Officer appointment:** A DPO must be appointed and registered with the relevant supervisory authority.

## Exceptions

Any deviation from the GDPR compliance checklist must be approved by the Data Protection Officer and documented with a risk acceptance rationale. Exceptions are reviewed quarterly.
