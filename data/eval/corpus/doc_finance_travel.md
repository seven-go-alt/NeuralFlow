# Meridian Analytics — Meal Expense Policy for One-Day Business Trips

**Document ID:** doc_finance_travel
**Owner:** Finance
**Last updated:** 2026-02-15

## Purpose

This policy clarifies whether employees are permitted to expense meal costs during a one-day business trip without an overnight stay. It establishes the rules and limits for meal expense reimbursement when an employee travels for business purposes and returns home the same day.

## Policy on one-day trip meals

Yes, employees are permitted to expense meal costs during a one-day business trip without an overnight stay. Meridian Analytics recognizes that business travel, even without an overnight stay, may require employees to purchase meals away from their usual work location. This policy provides reasonable reimbursement for those meals subject to the limits and conditions described below.

## Per-meal limits

For one-day business trips without an overnight stay, employees may expense meals up to the following per-meal limits:

- **Breakfast**: Up to $25 per meal
- **Lunch**: Up to $35 per meal
- **Dinner**: Up to $55 per meal

The combined daily cap for all meals on a one-day business trip is $115. These limits apply to the cost of food and non-alcoholic beverages only. Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable under any circumstances during a one-day business trip without an overnight stay. Tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the meal cost and count toward the per-meal limit.

## Conditions for reimbursement

To be eligible for reimbursement, meal expenses must meet all of the following conditions:

1. The employee is traveling on authorized Meridian business at least 50 miles from their primary work location or home office.
2. The travel is documented with a clear business purpose approved in advance by the employee's manager.
3. The meal is purchased during the period of authorized business travel.
4. The employee retains an itemized receipt for any single meal expense of $25 or more.
5. The expense is submitted within 30 days of the trip date using the standard expense report process.

## What is not covered

The following meal-related expenses are not reimbursable under this policy: meals consumed at the employee's primary work location, meals for family members or guests accompanying the traveler, alcoholic beverages, and meals provided as part of a conference or business meeting that the employer or event organizer already covers. If a client or business partner hosts the employee for a meal, the employee must not also expense that meal.

## Revision history

This policy was last updated on 15 February 2026. It supersedes all previous meal expense policies for one-day travel and will be reviewed annually.
