# Meridian Analytics — Insider Trading Compliance Policy

**Document ID:** doc_compliance_trading
**Owner:** Legal and Compliance
**Last updated:** 2026-01-22

## Purpose

This document explains how Meridian Analytics defines insider trading and what the reporting requirements are for employees who may possess material non-public information (MNPI). As a privately held company, Meridian does not have publicly traded securities, but employees may come into possession of MNPI about Meridian's customers, partners, or suppliers, and trading on that information is strictly prohibited.

## Definition of insider trading

Meridian Analytics defines insider trading as the purchase or sale of any security while in possession of material non-public information about that security's issuer, in violation of a duty of trust or confidence. The definition also includes tipping, which is the disclosure of material non-public information to another person who then trades on that information.

For the purposes of this policy, information is considered material if a reasonable investor would consider it important in making an investment decision. Information is considered non-public if it has not been broadly disseminated to the investing public through established channels. Examples of material non-public information that Meridian employees might encounter include:

- A major customer's decision to adopt Meridian's platform, indicating their growth trajectory
- A supplier's undisclosed financial difficulties that could affect their valuation
- A potential acquisition target being evaluated by Meridian's corporate development team
- Financial results or performance metrics of a customer or partner that have not been publicly released

## Reporting requirements

Employees who possess or become aware of material non-public information have the following reporting requirements under this compliance policy:

1. **Immediate disclosure to Legal:** Any employee who comes into possession of MNPI must report it to the Legal and Compliance team within 24 hours. The disclosure must include the nature of the information, how it was obtained, and the names of any individuals who may have also received the information.

2. **Trading restriction notification:** Upon receiving a report, the Legal team places the employee on a restricted trading list for any securities related to the MNPI. The employee is prohibited from trading those securities until the information becomes public or the Legal team determines the information is no longer material.

3. **Pre-clearance for insiders:** Employees in designated insider roles (executives, board members, and certain finance and corporate development roles) must obtain pre-clearance from Legal before trading in any securities. Pre-clearance requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before the proposed trade and are valid for 5 business days.

4. **Quarterly certification:** All employees in insider roles must complete a quarterly certification confirming that they have not traded on MNPI and are not aware of any unreported MNPI within their purview.

## Blackout periods

Meridian imposes quarterly blackout periods during which designated insiders may not trade in any securities. Blackout periods begin 15 days before the end of each fiscal quarter and end 48 hours after the public release of earnings for that quarter. The Legal team sends reminders 30 days before each blackout period begins.

## Consequences of non-compliance

Violation of this insider trading compliance policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment and referral to regulatory authorities for criminal prosecution. Insider trading violations can carry penalties of up to 20 years imprisonment and fines of up to $5 million for individuals.
