# Present Value Calculation for Lease Liability Under ASC 842

**Document ID:** MER-FIN-2026-006
**Owner:** Lease Accounting Advisory Team
**Last Updated:** July 15, 2026

## Guidance Reference

This memorandum addresses the appropriate methodology for calculating the present value of a lease liability under ASC 842 (Leases) when the implicit rate in the lease is not readily determinable. ASC 842-20-30-3 provides the framework for determining the discount rate that lessees should apply.

## Background

Under ASC 842, lessees are required to recognize a lease liability measured at the present value of the unpaid lease payments at the commencement date. The discount rate used to calculate present value has a significant impact on the reported lease liability and right-of-use asset amounts. ASC 842 prioritizes the use of the rate implicit in the lease but acknowledges that this rate is often not readily determinable for many lessees.

## When the Implicit Rate Is Not Readily Determinable

For many lease arrangements, particularly those involving specialized assets or leases where the lessor does not provide detailed pricing information, the implicit rate is not readily determinable. In such cases, ASC 842 requires the lessee to use its **incremental borrowing rate** as the discount rate.

The incremental borrowing rate is defined as the rate of interest that the lessee would have to pay to borrow on a collateralized basis over a similar term an amount equal to the lease payments in a similar economic environment.

## Determining the Incremental Borrowing Rate

Meridian Analytics determines its incremental borrowing rate by considering the following factors:

- **Lessee's credit rating:** Meridian's credit profile and any published credit ratings are the starting point for determining the rate.
- **Lease term:** The rate is specific to the lease term; a 3-year lease may have a different incremental borrowing rate than a 10-year lease.
- **Economic environment:** The rate reflects conditions in the economic environment where the lease is executed, including prevailing interest rates and market conditions.
- **Collateral:** Since lease obligations are effectively secured by the underlying asset, the incremental borrowing rate should reflect a collateralized borrowing rate.
- **Currency:** The rate should be denominated in the same currency as the lease payments.

## Practical Application at Meridian Analytics

Meridian calculates its incremental borrowing rate quarterly using a build-up approach:

1. **Base rate:** Start with the risk-free rate (e.g., US Treasury yield) for a term matching the lease term
2. **Credit spread:** Add Meridian's estimated credit spread based on its credit profile
3. **Collateral adjustment:** Apply an adjustment to reflect the secured nature of the lease obligation

The resulting rate is applied to discount the remaining lease payments to present value at lease commencement and at each subsequent reporting date if there is a modification or reassessment event.

## Disclosure Requirements

Under ASC 842, Meridian must disclose the discount rate used for lease calculations, including whether the rate is the implicit rate or the incremental borrowing rate, in the notes to the financial statements.
