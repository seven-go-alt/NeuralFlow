# Meridian Analytics — Engineering Organization Structure (2024–2025)

**Document ID:** doc_org_structure
**Owner:** Engineering
**Last updated:** 2026-01-30

## Overview

This document describes the evolution of the Meridian Analytics engineering organization between Q1 2024 and Q4 2025. This document details the number of engineering teams added during this timeframe and the rationale behind each addition.

## Engineering team structure in Q1 2024

At the start of Q1 2024, Meridian Analytics had four engineering teams:

1. **Core Platform Team:** Responsible for the main Meridian search and retrieval platform, including API gateway, authentication, and core infrastructure.
2. **Ingestion and Processing Team:** Focused on the document ingestion pipeline, file format support, and chunking strategies.
3. **Embedding and Search Team:** Owned the embedding generation pipeline, vector index management, and search relevance optimization.
4. **Frontend and Dashboard Team:** Built and maintained the customer-facing analytics dashboard and internal tooling.

These four teams comprised a total of 52 engineers at the beginning of Q1 2024.

## Engineering teams added between Q1 2024 and Q4 2025

Between Q1 2024 and Q4 2025, Meridian Analytics added five new engineering teams:

1. **Batch Processing Team (added Q2 2024):** Formed to develop and operate the batch processing endpoint, enabling high-throughput asynchronous document processing. This team was created in response to enterprise customer demand for large-scale nightly re-indexing workflows.

2. **Data Connectors Team (added Q3 2024):** Established to build and maintain pre-built integrations with third-party data sources including cloud storage providers, CRM systems, and industry-specific platforms such as healthcare EMR systems.

3. **Security and Compliance Engineering Team (added Q4 2024):** Created to address the growing regulatory and security requirements across Meridian's customer base. This team handles SOC 2 compliance automation, encryption infrastructure, access control systems, and security audit response.

4. **Machine Learning Platform Team (added Q1 2025):** Formed to manage the ML infrastructure powering the Embedding API v3, including model training pipelines, model serving infrastructure, and A/B testing frameworks for embedding quality improvements.

5. **Healthcare Vertical Team (added Q3 2025):** Established as a dedicated cross-functional engineering team to support the company's expansion into the healthcare vertical. The team develops HIPAA-compliant features, healthcare-specific data connectors, and clinical document processing capabilities.

## Engineering team count growth

In total, five engineering teams were added between Q1 2024 and Q4 2025. The engineering organization grew from 4 teams at the start of Q1 2024 to 9 teams by the end of Q4 2025. Headcount grew from 52 engineers to 138 engineers over the same period.

## Department alignment

Each engineering team is aligned with a product area and reports to the VP of Engineering. The VP of Engineering reports to the Chief Product Officer.
