# Meridian Analytics — Parental Leave Policy

**Document ID:** doc_hr_parental
**Owner:** People Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-04-01

## Purpose

This policy defines parental leave provisions for all full-time
employees of Meridian Analytics, including maternity leave duration
and the process for requesting extended leave. Meridian is committed
to supporting employees during the important life event of becoming
a parent.

## Scope and eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at
least 12 months of continuous service with Meridian Analytics.
Part-time employees with equivalent tenure are eligible on a pro-rated
basis. The policy covers birth parents, non-birthing parents, and
adoptive parents. Surrogacy arrangements are treated equivalently to
birth for the intended parent.

## Maternity leave duration

Eligible employees giving birth are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid
maternity leave. The leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected
due date. Employees must have been employed with Meridian for at least
12 months to qualify for paid maternity leave. Leave taken before the
birth counts toward the total 16-week entitlement.

## Partner and adoption leave

Non-birthing parents are eligible for 8 weeks of paid parental leave,
to be taken within the first 12 months following the child's birth or
placement. Adoption leave follows the same entitlement as partner
leave. Both parents adopting jointly may each take the full 8-week
entitlement. Employees adopting a child may begin leave on the date
of placement or the date the child enters the home, whichever is
earlier.

## Extended leave request process

Employees who require time beyond the standard maternity leave duration
may submit a request for extended leave. The **request process** is as
follows:

1. Submit a written request to People Operations at least 30 days before
   the planned end of standard maternity leave.
2. Include expected additional duration and reason for the extension.
3. Obtain approval from both the employee's director and the VP of
   People Operations.
4. Extended leave beyond the initial 16 weeks is typically granted in
   increments of up to 4 additional weeks (unpaid), for a maximum total
   of 20 weeks.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed the same role or
an equivalent position. Meridian also offers a phased return option,
allowing part-time hours at full pay for the first 4 weeks back.
Employees should notify People Operations of their intended return
date at least 4 weeks in advance.

## Coordination with other leave

Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory
parental pay or leave entitlements. Employees may also choose to
combine parental leave with accrued annual leave after the parental
leave period ends. Annual leave entitlement continues to accrue
during parental leave. Refer to the Annual Leave Policy (doc_hr_leave)
for accrual details.

## FAQ

**Q: Can parental leave be taken intermittently?**
A: No. Parental leave must be taken in a continuous block unless an
alternative schedule is approved in advance by People Operations.

**Q: Are pregnancy-related medical appointments covered?**
A: Yes. Time off for antenatal appointments is not counted against
maternity leave. Employees should notify their manager and log these
appointments separately.
