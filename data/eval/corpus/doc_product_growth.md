# Meridian Analytics — Fiscal Year 2025 Product Growth Report

**Document ID:** doc_product_growth
**Owner:** Product Management
**Last updated:** 2026-02-15

## Overview

This document summarizes the year-over-year growth performance of Meridian Analytics products during the last fiscal year (FY 2025, ending 31 December 2025). The report identifies which products had a year-over-year growth rate exceeding 20% in the last fiscal year and analyzes the drivers behind that growth.

## Year-over-year growth metrics

Meridian Analytics measures product growth using annual recurring revenue (ARR) as the primary metric. Year-over-year growth is calculated by comparing the ARR at the end of FY 2025 against the ARR at the end of FY 2024. The following table presents the year-over-year growth rates for each product line:

| Product | FY 2024 ARR | FY 2025 ARR | Year-over-year growth |
|---------|-------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Meridian Search Core | $18.2M | $22.4M | 23.1% |
| Meridian Embedding Engine | $7.5M | $11.8M | 57.3% |
| Meridian Document Ingestion | $9.1M | $10.7M | 17.6% |
| Meridian Analytics Dashboard | $5.3M | $6.1M | 15.1% |
| Meridian Batch Processing | $3.8M | $4.6M | 21.1% |
| Meridian Data Connectors | $2.1M | $2.8M | 33.3% |

## Products with year-over-year growth exceeding 20%

The following products had a year-over-year growth rate exceeding 20% in the last fiscal year:

1. **Meridian Embedding Engine (57.3%):** This product experienced the highest growth, driven by the launch of Embedding API v3, which introduced multilingual support and larger dimension sizes. The embedding market expanded significantly as more enterprises adopted RAG architectures.

2. **Meridian Data Connectors (33.3%):** Growth was fueled by the addition of 12 new connectors for industry-specific data sources, including healthcare EMR systems and financial trading platforms.

3. **Meridian Search Core (23.1%):** Steady growth driven by enterprise tier adoption and the introduction of hybrid search capabilities combining keyword and vector search.

4. **Meridian Batch Processing (21.1%):** Growth was supported by the batch processing endpoint's throughput improvements, enabling customers to process larger volumes of documents asynchronously.

Products that did not exceed the 20% threshold include Meridian Document Ingestion (17.6%) and Meridian Analytics Dashboard (15.1%). The overall portfolio year-over-year growth rate for FY 2025 was 25.8%, up from 22.1% in FY 2024.

## Growth drivers

Several strategic initiatives contributed to the strong year-over-year growth performance. The expansion of the enterprise sales team from 12 to 18 account executives increased pipeline coverage in the healthcare and financial services verticals. The introduction of usage-based pricing for the Embedding Engine attracted mid-market customers who previously found the per-seat model cost-prohibitive. Additionally, the launch of the Meridian Partner Program in Q2 2025 generated 47 new reseller-led deals during the fiscal year.

## Outlook for FY 2026

Based on the current pipeline and product roadmap, the Product Management team projects an overall year-over-year growth rate of 30% for FY 2026. The Embedding Engine is expected to remain the highest-growth product, with a projected growth rate of 65%.
